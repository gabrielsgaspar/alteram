---
id: case-007
title: Clutch replaced by the claimant's garage nine months after supply of a used car on conditional sale
kind: balanced
expected: respondent
---

[1] Mr G (the claimant) complains about Ferncliff Finance (the respondent).

[2] On 20 January 2026 Mr G signed a conditional sale agreement with Ferncliff Finance for a used city car, arranged through the dealer Harrow Lane Motors.

[3] Mr G collected the car from Harrow Lane Motors on 20 January 2026.

[4] When it was supplied, the car was four years old, had 31,500 miles on the clock and had a cash price of £8,900.

[5] On 12 October 2026, at 40,200 miles, the clutch began to slip and the car struggled to climb hills.

[6] Mr G did not tell Ferncliff Finance about the fault before having it repaired.

[7] On 15 October 2026 Mr G's local garage replaced the clutch and charged him £780.

[8] The garage's invoice describes the old clutch friction plate as "burnt and glazed".

[9] The garage disposed of the old clutch, and only Mr G's photographs of it remain.

[10] On 16 October 2026 Mr G asked Ferncliff Finance to refund the £780.

[11] Ferncliff Finance's technical adviser reviewed the invoice and the photographs.

[12] The adviser's written note says a burnt and glazed friction plate is usually caused by slipping the clutch while driving, and is not evidence of a fault present at supply.

[13] Mr G says he drives normally.

[14] Mr G says a clutch on this model should last well beyond 40,000 miles.

[15] Ferncliff Finance says it was given no chance to inspect or repair the car, and has refused to pay.
