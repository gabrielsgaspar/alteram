---
id: case-006
title: Recorded mileage of a used car on conditional sale questioned six weeks after supply
kind: one-sided
expected: claimant
---

[1] Ms F (the claimant) complains about Ashgrove Motor Finance (the respondent).

[2] On 2 July 2026 Ms F signed a conditional sale agreement with Ashgrove Motor Finance for a used estate car, arranged through the dealer Pennant Autos.

[3] Ms F collected the car from Pennant Autos on 2 July 2026.

[4] The dealer's advertisement and the agreement both describe the car as having 38,000 miles on the clock.

[5] When it was supplied, the car was five years old and had a cash price of £15,750.

[6] On 14 August 2026 Ms F looked up the car's MOT history on the government's online service.

[7] The MOT history records 61,240 miles at the car's MOT test on 20 May 2025.

[8] On 14 August 2026 the car's odometer showed 39,900 miles.

[9] On 15 August 2026 Ms F wrote to Ashgrove Motor Finance asking to return the car or, if that was refused, for a price reduction.

[10] A valuation obtained by Ms F from Hartley Valuations says that in July 2026 this model, at this age and in this condition, was worth about £2,300 less with 62,000 miles than with 38,000 miles.

[11] Ashgrove Motor Finance says it relied on the dealer's description and did not know the mileage was wrong.

[12] Ashgrove Motor Finance says more than 30 days had passed before Ms F asked to return the car.

[13] Ashgrove Motor Finance has offered Ms F £300 as a goodwill payment.

[14] Ms F asks to return the car and end the agreement or, in the alternative, a price reduction of £2,300.
