---
id: case-005
title: Engine failure two months after supply of a used car on hire purchase
kind: one-sided
expected: respondent
---

[1] Mr E (the claimant) complains about Larkfield Finance (the respondent).

[2] On 9 June 2026 Mr E signed a hire purchase agreement with Larkfield Finance for a used hatchback, arranged through the dealer Moorgate Car Sales.

[3] Mr E collected the car from Moorgate Car Sales on 9 June 2026.

[4] When it was supplied, the car was three years old, had 24,800 miles on the clock and had a cash price of £13,400.

[5] On 18 August 2026 the engine stopped while Mr E was driving, and it would not restart.

[6] Mr E says that shortly before the engine stopped he drove through a stretch of road with standing water after heavy rain.

[7] On 22 August 2026 an engineer from Bridgewell Vehicle Reports, instructed jointly by Mr E and Larkfield Finance, examined the car.

[8] The engineer's report says water entered the engine through the air intake and caused hydraulic lock, which bent two connecting rods.

[9] The engineer's report says this damage is consistent with driving through water deep enough to reach the air intake.

[10] The engineer's report says the air intake on this model sits 38 cm above the ground.

[11] The engineer's report says it found no fault in the engine or the air intake that could have caused the failure without water entering from outside.

[12] Mr E says the water was no more than a few centimetres deep.

[13] Mr E asks Larkfield Finance to pay for a replacement engine, quoted at £4,900.

[14] Larkfield Finance says the damage was caused by water entering the engine on the day it failed, not by any fault present when the car was supplied, and has refused to pay.
