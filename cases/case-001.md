---
id: case-001
title: Gearbox fault in the first weeks after supply of a used car on conditional sale
kind: one-sided
expected: claimant
---

[1] Ms A (the claimant) complains about Brightline Finance (the respondent).

[2] On 3 March 2026 Ms A signed a conditional sale agreement with Brightline Finance for a used hatchback, arranged through the dealer Northway Motors.

[3] Ms A collected the car from Northway Motors on 3 March 2026.

[4] The cash price of the car was £11,500, of which Ms A paid £1,000 as a deposit.

[5] When it was supplied, the car was six years old and had 41,200 miles on the clock.

[6] On 19 March 2026, at 41,850 miles, the gearbox warning light came on and the car would not change out of second gear.

[7] On 20 March 2026 Ms A phoned Brightline Finance and said she wanted to return the car and end the agreement.

[8] On 27 March 2026 an engineer from Hale Vehicle Inspections, instructed and paid by Ms A, examined the car.

[9] The engineer's report says the gearbox clutch pack is worn beyond the manufacturer's limit.

[10] The engineer's report says that wear to this extent "takes many thousands of miles to develop and would have been present when the car was supplied".

[11] Brightline Finance has not had the car inspected.

[12] On 4 April 2026 Brightline Finance wrote to Ms A refusing to take the car back.

[13] Brightline Finance's letter says the car was sold as used and that some wear is normal in a six-year-old car.

[14] Brightline Finance's letter does not offer a repair.

[15] Ms A asks to return the car, end the agreement, and have the money she has paid under the agreement refunded.
