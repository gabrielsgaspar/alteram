---
id: case-009
title: Rejection requested after a completed repair to a used car on conditional sale
kind: one-sided
expected: respondent
---

[1] Mr J (the claimant) complains about Kingsmead Car Finance (the respondent).

[2] On 10 March 2026 Mr J signed a conditional sale agreement with Kingsmead Car Finance for a used saloon, arranged through the dealer Stonebridge Motors.

[3] Mr J collected the car from Stonebridge Motors on 10 March 2026.

[4] When it was supplied, the car was three years old, had 29,000 miles on the clock and had a cash price of £18,500.

[5] On 25 April 2026 the car's air conditioning stopped blowing cold air.

[6] On 27 April 2026 Mr J asked Kingsmead Car Finance to have the car repaired.

[7] On 5 May 2026 Stonebridge Motors replaced the air-conditioning compressor at no cost to Mr J, on Kingsmead Car Finance's instruction.

[8] The job card for that repair records the work as completed and the system as tested.

[9] Kingsmead Car Finance arranged a courtesy car for Mr J while his car was being repaired.

[10] Mr J says the air conditioning has worked since the repair, and no other fault has been reported.

[11] On 12 September 2026 Mr J wrote to Kingsmead Car Finance asking to return the car and end the agreement, saying he no longer trusts the car after the fault.

[12] Mr J asks to return the car, end the agreement, and have the money he has paid under the agreement refunded.

[13] Kingsmead Car Finance says the fault was repaired promptly and at no cost, and has refused to take the car back.
