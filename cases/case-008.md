---
id: case-008
title: Automatic gearbox fault seven months after supply of a used car on hire purchase
kind: missing-fact
expected: undecidable
---

[1] Ms H (the claimant) complains about Orchard Vehicle Finance (the respondent).

[2] On 4 February 2026 Ms H signed a hire purchase agreement with Orchard Vehicle Finance for a used SUV, arranged through the dealer Westbrook Cars.

[3] Ms H collected the car from Westbrook Cars on 4 February 2026.

[4] When it was supplied, the car was five years old, had 47,000 miles on the clock and had a cash price of £16,200.

[5] On 15 September 2026, at 55,300 miles, the automatic gearbox began to jerk when changing gear, and a warning message appeared on the dashboard.

[6] On 16 September 2026 Ms H reported the fault to Orchard Vehicle Finance by email.

[7] On 29 September 2026 an engineer from Castlegate Inspections, instructed jointly by Ms H and Orchard Vehicle Finance, examined the car.

[8] On 6 October 2026 Castlegate Inspections sent its written report to both parties.

[9] Neither party has provided a copy of the report.

[10] Ms H says the engineer told her at the inspection that the gearbox had a manufacturing defect.

[11] Orchard Vehicle Finance says the report shows the fault is caused by wear.

[12] The service history shows the car was serviced on schedule, including a gearbox oil change in April 2026.

[13] Ms H asks Orchard Vehicle Finance to pay for a gearbox repair, quoted at £3,600.

[14] Orchard Vehicle Finance has refused to pay.
