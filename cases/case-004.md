---
id: case-004
title: Front brake wear ten months after supply of a used car on conditional sale
kind: one-sided
expected: respondent
---

[1] Mr D (the claimant) complains about Ashby Vehicle Finance (the respondent).

[2] On 14 November 2025 Mr D signed a conditional sale agreement with Ashby Vehicle Finance for a used saloon, arranged through the dealer Calder Motors.

[3] Mr D collected the car from Calder Motors on 14 November 2025.

[4] When it was supplied, the car was five years old, had 48,500 miles on the clock and had a cash price of £12,900.

[5] The pre-delivery inspection sheet, signed by Mr D at collection, records the front brake pads at 6 mm and the front brake discs as within the manufacturer's limit.

[6] On 22 September 2026, at 67,800 miles, Mr D heard a grinding noise from the front of the car when braking.

[7] On 25 September 2026 Mr D emailed Ashby Vehicle Finance about the noise.

[8] On 6 October 2026 an engineer from Pennine Vehicle Assessors, instructed jointly by Mr D and Ashby Vehicle Finance, examined the car.

[9] The engineer's report says the front brake pads are worn down to their metal backing plates and the front discs are scored.

[10] The engineer's report says the wear is consistent with the roughly 19,000 miles the car has covered since supply, and that it found no defect in the braking system.

[11] Mr D says he noticed no warning signs before 22 September 2026.

[12] Mr D says brakes on a car that cost £12,900 should last longer than ten months.

[13] Mr D asks Ashby Vehicle Finance to pay for new front brake pads and discs, which a local garage has quoted at £480.

[14] Ashby Vehicle Finance says brake pads and discs wear with use, that their condition at supply was recorded, and has refused to pay.
