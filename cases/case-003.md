---
id: case-003
title: Clutch fault around six months after supply of a used car on conditional sale
kind: missing-fact
expected: undecidable
---

[1] Ms C (the claimant) complains about Harbour Lane Finance (the respondent).

[2] On 2 September 2025 Ms C signed a conditional sale agreement with Harbour Lane Finance for a used small SUV, arranged through the dealer Elmfield Autos.

[3] Ms C collected the car from Elmfield Autos on 2 September 2025.

[4] When it was supplied, the car was seven years old, had 63,000 miles on the clock and had a cash price of £9,800.

[5] Ms C says the clutch began to slip in mid-February 2026.

[6] Ms C says she took the car to Elmfield Autos' workshop about the slipping clutch before the end of February 2026.

[7] Ms C says the workshop recorded that visit on a job card.

[8] Neither party has provided a copy of the job card.

[9] Harbour Lane Finance says the first report of a clutch fault it knows of is an email from Ms C on 20 March 2026.

[10] On 20 March 2026 the car had 69,400 miles on the clock.

[11] On 8 April 2026 an engineer from Coastline Vehicle Reports, instructed jointly by Ms C and Harbour Lane Finance, examined the car.

[12] The engineer's report says the clutch friction plate is worn.

[13] The engineer's report says it is not possible to tell from the wear alone whether the fault was present when the car was supplied.

[14] Ms C asks Harbour Lane Finance to pay for a replacement clutch, quoted at £1,100.

[15] Harbour Lane Finance says clutch wear is normal on a seven-year-old car, and has refused to pay.
